**Subject:** Thank you – a free Vietnamese storytelling practice tool inspired by your channel

Hi Philipp,

My name is Minh Tuấn. I run Quản trị tử tế ("Kind Management"), a small Vietnamese website with free tools for office workers and small business owners.

Your videos and the "5 Days to Great Stories" emails changed how I think about storytelling, especially zooming into one moment and the habit of noting one moment every day.

Most people in Vietnam can't follow English videos easily, so I built a free practice tool in Vietnamese: a 28-day path, 10 minutes a day, with three tracks (everyday conversation, speaking on camera, and presenting at work). Every lesson is written in our own words with our own Vietnamese office examples, and each one credits you and links back to your original video. The tool clearly states that it is not affiliated with you. For extra practice in English, it points people to speaking.games.

Here it is: https://quantritute.com/tools/storytelling/

I just wanted to say thank you. If anything in the tool doesn't sit right with you, how we credit you or any part of the content, please tell me and I'll change it.
